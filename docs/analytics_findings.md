# Creator database analytics

All findings below describe the 10-record synthetic demonstration sample. They are not production metrics.

## Dashboard metrics

- Creators: 10
- Average profile completeness: 81.3 / 100
- Creators with at least one verified collaboration: 80%
- Active, verified, High-severity risks: 1

## Findings

1. Beauty is the largest category with 3 creators; Tech has only 1 creator and is the clearest sample coverage gap.
2. Product Reviews is the most represented tag, appearing across 3 creators.
3. Verified collaboration coverage is 100% for Fashion, Fitness, and Tech, 66.7% for Beauty, and 50% for Lifestyle.
4. Market is missing for 10% of creator profiles. The other selected core creator fields have no missing values.
5. The risk table contains 2 Low-, 1 Medium-, and 1 High-severity records. Only one record is simultaneously High, Verified, and Active.

## Interpretation limits

- The dataset is intentionally small and synthetic.
- Category percentages should not be interpreted as market supply.
- Collaboration coverage measures recorded, verified collaboration history, not creator quality or campaign performance.
- Missing risk records do not prove that a creator has no real-world risk.
- These metrics demonstrate an analytics workflow and help identify data-collection priorities.
